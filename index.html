Owner Story

Meet Mark.

My name is Mark, and I’m the owner of M&M Detailing, proudly serving Ruskin, Florida.

I started this business in 2022, but my passion for detailing goes back much further. Around age 14, I spent time working in my uncle’s classic car restoration shop, helping bring vehicles back to life and handling the detailing after restorations were complete.

Seeing those cars transformed — sometimes better than their original condition — is what made me fall in love with this work.

As someone with a military background, I’ve built this business on discipline, precision, and attention to detail. I take pride in delivering high-end results on every vehicle I touch, whether it’s a daily driver, a work truck, or a luxury or classic car. I don’t believe in cutting corners — I focus on the small details that make a big difference.

Because of my military background, I also have a deep respect for the people who serve our communities. That’s why I proudly offer discounts to military members, veterans, first responders, medical personnel, and educators.

— Mark, Owner

Good To Know

Frequently asked questions.

Still have a question? Call or text (813) 419-9026 and Mark will help.

Do you really come to me?

Yes — we're a fully mobile service. We detail at your home, office, or wherever your vehicle is parked, throughout Ruskin, Sun City Center, Apollo Beach, Wimauma, and Riverview.

How long does a detail take?

It depends on the service and vehicle condition. A standard interior or exterior detail is typically a couple of hours; full details and paint correction take longer. We'll give you a time estimate when you book.

How do I pay?

We accept cash, card, and major payment apps. For gift cards, you can request one online and we'll confirm payment by phone, text, or your preferred method.

Do you offer any discounts?

We proudly offer discounts to military members, veterans, first responders, medical personnel, and educators. Just mention it when you book.

How do I book?

The easiest way is to call or text (813) 419-9026. You can also email us or grab a gift card online any time.
